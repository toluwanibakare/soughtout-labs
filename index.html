Our Services

Comprehensive Diagnostic Services

Professional laboratory and radiology investigations organised into clear categories, delivered at your convenience.

Haematology

Blood investigations including Full Blood Count (FBC) and other blood-related studies.

Microbiology

Urine, swabs, aspirates, cultures, microscopy and related diagnostic procedures.

Serology

HIV screening, Hepatitis A, B and C testing, VDRL/Syphilis and other immune-related tests.

Chemical Pathology

Electrolytes, hormone profiles, liver and kidney function tests, and related studies.
