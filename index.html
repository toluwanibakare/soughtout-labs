Our Services

Comprehensive Diagnostic Services

Professional laboratory and radiology investigations organised into clear categories, delivered at your convenience.

Haematology

Blood investigations including Full Blood Count (FBC) and other blood-related studies.

Microbiology

Investigations involving urine, swabs, aspirates, cultures, microscopy and related diagnostic procedures.

Serology

HIV screening, Hepatitis A, B and C testing, VDRL/Syphilis testing and other immune-related investigations.

Chemical Pathology

Electrolytes, hormone profiles, liver function tests, enzymes, kidney function tests and related studies.

Histology & Cytology

Professional examination of tissues and cells to support accurate diagnosis.

Molecular & Special Tests

Advanced investigations including PCR, HbA1c and other specialised diagnostic tests.

Radiology Services

Professional imaging and radiology-related services based on patient needs.
